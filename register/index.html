Why This Matters

The primary is the real election

In most Utah districts, the Republican nominee wins the general election. That means the primary decides who represents you. If you're not registered to vote in it, someone else makes that choice for you.

🔒

Closed primary

Only registered Republicans receive a Republican primary ballot. No exceptions.

📅

April 1 is the deadline

If you're currently registered with another party, you must switch by April 1 to participate. After that, you're locked out.

⏱️

2 minutes, online

Go to vote.utah.gov with your driver's license number. That's all you need. No forms to mail, no office to visit.

How To Do It

Three steps. Two minutes.

Go to vote.utah.gov

Click "Register to Vote or Update Registration" on the state voter portal.

Enter your information

You'll need your Utah driver's license or state ID number. If you don't have one, the last four digits of your Social Security number will work.

Select "Republican" as your party

Update your party affiliation and submit. You'll receive confirmation. That's it — you're in.

Common Questions

You might be wondering

What if I'm unaffiliated / independent?
Good news — unaffiliated voters can affiliate with a party at any time without the April 1 deadline restriction. But we'd recommend doing it now so there are no surprises when ballots go out in June.
Can I switch back after the primary?
Yes. You can change your party affiliation at any time. Just keep in mind that if you switch away from Republican and want to switch back later, the same annual deadline will apply.
Why is the Republican primary closed?
The Utah Republican Party requires voters to be registered with the party to participate in their primary elections. The Democratic Party holds an open primary. This is a party decision, not a state law.
I'm already registered Republican. Do I need to do anything?
Nope — you're all set. You'll automatically receive your primary ballot by mail in early June. But you can help by sharing this page with friends and neighbors who might need to update their registration.
What is House District 14?
Utah House District 14 is a state legislative seat. Your state representative votes on education funding, taxes, public safety and the issues that affect your daily life more directly than any federal office.
