HD14 at a Glance

House District 14 covers Clearfield and Syracuse in Davis County. Here are the numbers that shape the policy conversation.

~42,000
Population
Residents of House District 14, primarily in Clearfield and Syracuse.
26%
School-Age Children (5–17)
Compared to the state average of 22%. HD14 is a young-family district.
~$515K
Median Home Price
Housing costs in the district continue to rise, straining young families and first-time buyers.
~67,000
Students Served
Students across the Davis School District — Utah’s 2nd largest. Kara serves them daily as ED of Davis Education Foundation.
103
Schools
Schools across the Davis School District, serving communities from Bountiful to Clearfield.
Last
Per-Pupil Spending Rank
Utah ranks last in the nation for per-pupil education spending. HD14 families feel this every day.

The statistics on this page are drawn from publicly available data to help District 14 residents understand the community they live in and the challenges their next representative will face.
